Operational KPI Design and Performance Reporting

Example data shown for portfolio presentation only; figures do not represent actual values from any organisation.

What I Did

  • Redesigned the KPI measurement approach using case outcomes and a new reference structure
  • Built the Power BI semantic model for outcome-based volume and percentage reporting
  • Developed DAX measures for KPI achievement, case volumes, proportional reporting and filter-context handling
  • Designed report pages showing headline performance, trend movement and outcome detail
  • Created supporting definitions and calculation notes so stakeholders could understand the measure consistently

Context

The organisation needed a reliable way to report a significant internal and external KPI across both the percentage achieved and the underlying case volumes. The metric was visible to operational leadership, clinical contact centre teams, board-level reporting and external stakeholders, so accuracy and a clear definition were essential.

The previous calculation relied on keyword-based logic that was vulnerable to wording variation and changes in operational recording. The project required a more stable outcome-based measurement approach, a clear reporting structure and a Power BI report that could show performance over time while still allowing users to understand which case outcomes contributed to the figure.

Key Reporting Challenges

  • Replacing keyword-based logic with a more accurate outcome-based KPI definition
  • Showing both KPI percentage and absolute case volume without confusing the two views
  • Making group and outcome detail visible for operational review and challenge
  • Supporting senior reporting while still allowing detailed investigation of contributing cases
  • Documenting the calculation clearly enough for stakeholders and data teams to apply it consistently

Data Model

The data model used case-level outcome information supported by a new reference table that defined which outcomes contributed to the KPI. This replaced the earlier approach of searching for keywords in recorded values and made the measure more resilient to operational language changes.

A SQL view brought together the relevant case, team and outcome fields so the Power BI model could report consistently across headline performance, outcome breakdown and operational group detail. DAX measures used filter-aware totals, division handling and context management so the same definitions could be used across cards, trends, tables and bookmarked views.

Report Design

The report was designed around a simple performance narrative: current KPI position, movement over time, volume context and the operational detail behind the result. Users could switch between percentage and volume views using bookmarks, helping senior leaders review performance without losing sight of the volume of cases behind the metric.

Outcome and group-level detail provided the operational context needed to understand changes in the headline figure. A floating information window and supporting notes explained the metric and helped users interpret the calculation correctly.

Outcome

The redesigned KPI approach produced a more accurate and defensible performance measure. Moving from keyword matching to outcome-based reference logic reduced ambiguity and made the metric easier to explain, validate and maintain.

The Power BI report gave senior and operational stakeholders a clear view of performance over time, the volume behind the percentage and the outcome categories driving the result. The same calculation logic could be reused for formal reporting and operational review without maintaining separate manual extracts.
