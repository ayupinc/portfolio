What Maple Leaf does

From a difficult operational question to a view the service can use.

The work joins up operational understanding, careful analysis and practical reporting. The method stays in the background; the service question stays in view.

01

Understand the service

Clarify the operational question, map how work really moves and agree definitions that make sense to the people running the service.

02

Create a trusted picture

Bring fragmented operational information together into a clear account of demand, queues, activity, capacity and performance.

03

Put it into use

Deliver practical reporting and decision support that teams can use for live oversight, performance review and service improvement.

Selected case studies

Operational problems, resolved in practice.

Each one-page study is written for operational leaders: the organisation's challenge, what Maple Leaf did and what changed.

01

Clinical operations · 24/7 service

Creating a live, trusted view of clinical queues

The challenge

A clinical contact centre could not reliably see how many cases were waiting, how long they had waited or where pressure was building.

Maple Leaf's response

Maple Leaf reconstructed the service's queue history and turned it into one near-live operational view for team leaders.

What changed

Manual checks were replaced by a shared view of waiting demand, active work and the longest waits across the service.

Continuous operational useOpen one-page case study ↓
02

Contact centre · Demand and workforce

Building one account of calls, service and workforce activity

The challenge

High-volume call and workforce information was difficult to reconcile, leaving operational teams without one dependable account of demand and delivery.

Maple Leaf's response

Maple Leaf rebuilt the reporting around the organisation's own records and created a consistent view from service demand to individual activity.

What changed

Five reports now support demand planning, service performance, workload review and detailed operational investigation.

2.5m calls represented annuallyOpen one-page case study ↓
03

Performance management · Waiting time

Defining a waiting-time measure the service could trust

The challenge

A seemingly simple waiting-time question produced different answers depending on which workflow events were used.

Maple Leaf's response

Maple Leaf worked back from the operational meaning of the measure, agreed the right start and end points and made exceptions visible.

What changed

Leaders gained a transparent measure that showed typical waits, variation and the cases behind the headline performance.
